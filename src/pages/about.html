The shop

We do the whole
job ourselves

Most shops do one thing. If you need a boat's interior rebuilt and a new tower fabricated and the hardware coated, you're usually managing three vendors, three schedules and three people blaming each other.

We cut and weld the metal, stitch the upholstery, and coat the finish — in the same building, by the same people. One quote, one timeline, one shop answering the phone.

It also means the trades talk to each other. The person building your frame knows what the upholsterer needs from it, because they're ten feet apart.

What comes through the door

Boats, mostly.
But not only.

Marine work is the backbone — pontoons, bass boats, deck boats, center consoles. Around that sits everything else a metal-and-fabric shop gets asked for.

  1. Marine upholstery & fabrication

    Full interiors, helm seating, bench rebuilds, aluminium towers, T-tops, leaning posts, consoles and tanks.

  2. Vehicle interiors

    Classic car restorations, ATV and side-by-side seats, motorhome and RV interiors — diamond stitch a speciality.

  3. Gates, railings & architectural metal

    Driveway gates, fence panels, handrails, stair and walkway railings, gangways.

  4. Signs & cut metal art

    CNC plasma-cut signage and medallions — ranch entrances, business signs, family pieces.

  5. Powder coating

    Wheels, frames, railings and hardware, blasted and cured in-house.
