Powder Coating

A finish that
outlasts paint

Durable, corrosion-resistant and affordable. Unlike paint, powder coating lasts longer, resists chips and needs almost no upkeep, which matters on anything that lives outdoors or on the water. Blasted, coated and cured in-house, in a range of colours and textures.
