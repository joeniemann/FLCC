FinishLines Creative Concepts  ·  Cut & Sewn

One outline,
two tools

The identity runs on a single premise: every surface belongs to either the plasma torch or the needle, and the seam between them is draggable. Fabrication holds the left — heat, chrome and white-hot light. Upholstery holds the right — marine vinyl, saddle orange and pure white thread. This guide documents the system as built across all five pages.

02

Surfaces

The hero committed to a single dark world. Services needs dark (fabrication), light (upholstery) and mixed (powder coating), so surfaces became a band-level concern. .band--dark and .band--light redefine a small set of tokens; every component reads only those and never a raw colour. Add a band class and the whole section re-skins.

Forms

Both forms validate client-side and report status, but deliberately do not transmit — no endpoint is wired yet. Each is marked in the source with exactly what to change. Validation runs over the form's real controls, not only fields wrapped in .field, so a bare input still validates.

09

Motion

Long ambient loops, short sharp interaction responses. Everything eases on cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1) — fast out, settled landing, no bouncing. Every looping animation is gated by an IntersectionObserver so it costs nothing off screen, and all of it is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

11

Rules & open items

Fabrication left, upholstery right

Fixed everywhere. Steel, chrome and the torch on the left of the seam; vinyl, thread and the needle on the right. Photography focal points, veil directions and the tool's side test all follow from it.

One font, no exceptions

No mono, no serif, no second family. If something needs to read as a label, change case and tracking — not typeface.

Components read tokens, never colours

A component that hardcodes #08090A breaks the moment it lands on a light band. Read --bg, --fg, --accent.

Scope page-specific CSS

hero.css loads after site.css; an unscoped .eyebrow there once repainted every eyebrow on the homepage. Page stylesheets scope to their own root.

Degrade to finished

With no JS, reduced motion, or in a background tab, animated type renders complete rather than blank. Animation reveals a finished state; it is never the only way to see it.

Measure, don't eyeball

Layout claims are checked with tools/responsive-test.html across 60 page/width combinations before they are called done.

Scale every dimension by one factor

Moving a lockup into a new coordinate space means scaling height, type size and detail by the same factor as the width. Scaling width alone is what turned the tagline into a thin bar with the words floating in it.

Effects are ratios, not pixels

An emboss, a glow or a shadow has to hold at every render size. Express the offset as a fraction of the glyph or shape it decorates — the tagline's raised lettering broke at 13% of cap height and works at 6%.
